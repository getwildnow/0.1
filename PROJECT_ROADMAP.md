# PRIME CARE - Project Roadmap

## Company: Global Care | Product: PRIME CARE

---

## 🎯 Big Picture

We're an **Applied AI Company** building a complete health data ecosystem. We collect comprehensive personal data (health records, wearables, social media, interviews) and apply AI to make it useful for the customer.

**Core Innovation:** We only make money when we PAY claims (10% service fee), not when we deny them.

## 📊 Key Metrics to Track

- **Customer Acquisition:** Sign-ups per week
- **Claim Processing Time:** Must stay under 59 minutes
- **Support Response Time:** Must stay under 30 seconds
- **Customer Satisfaction:** NPS score
- **Claim Approval Rate:** Should be near 100% (we cover everything)
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**
- **Churn Rate:** Should be low (no lock-in)

## 🎨 Design Principles

1. **Simplicity First:** Everything should be dead simple
2. **Speed:** Every action should feel instant
3. **Transparency:** Show everything clearly
4. **Trust:** Premium look & feel, build confidence
5. **Mobile-First:** Most users will be on mobile
6. **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
